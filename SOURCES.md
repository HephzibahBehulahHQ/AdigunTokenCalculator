# Sources and calculation methodology

This file records the external references and explicit assumptions used by the browser-side tools. All calculations run locally. A source reference does not imply a live connection.

## Tokenization

- OpenAI's [tiktoken repository](https://github.com/openai/tiktoken) documents the encodings and model mappings. The OpenAI Cookbook's [token-counting guide](https://github.com/openai/openai-cookbook/blob/main/examples/How_to_count_tokens_with_tiktoken.ipynb) maps GPT-4o and GPT-4o mini to `o200k_base`, and GPT-4 / GPT-3.5 Turbo to `cl100k_base`.
- The current upstream [model mapping source](https://github.com/openai/tiktoken/blob/main/tiktoken/model.py) is reviewed when model families change.
- The app bundles [`tiktoken@1.0.22`](https://github.com/dqbd/tiktoken/tree/4c8b748e07992c00386f3180af5c574b27b65139), its local WASM runtime, and only the `cl100k_base` / `o200k_base` rank data. The supported public `decode_single_token_bytes()` API reconstructs token bytes with a streaming UTF-8 decoder. It counts the exact supplied text, including whitespace, literal special-token strings and Unicode, not message framing, tools, images, audio, or API billing units. Tokenization runs in a local background worker; the UI displays 2,000 tokens per page while copy actions include the complete result. See [architecture](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md).

## Population and religion

- World population estimates use UN DESA, [World Population Prospects 2024](https://population.un.org/wpp/), medium-variant 1 July estimates. The app linearly interpolates between dated mid-year projections and labels the result as an estimate, not a live count. Births, deaths and CO₂ are omitted because no separate verified daily baselines are shipped.
- Religion shares are seeded from Pew Research Center, [How the global religious landscape changed from 2010 to 2020](https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/2025/06/09/how-the-global-religious-landscape-changed-from-2010-to-2020/), published 9 June 2025. Its census, survey and register sources classify religious identity; questions, methods and regional patterns vary. Shares are editable; the “other religions” aggregate includes folk/traditional and smaller traditions. Unaffiliated means no stated religious affiliation, not a claim about personal beliefs. The UI distinguishes unchanged source shares from edited entries. Exact decimal totals determine below/equal/above 100%; input is never silently normalized. Counts equal the entered population × share / 100, rounded for display. This educational tool endorses no belief system.

## Currency and finance

- Offline currency rates are the European Central Bank's [euro foreign exchange reference rates](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/policy_and_exchange_rates/euro_reference_exchange_rates/html/index.en.html), snapshot dated **4 November 2026**. The app stores EUR as 1 and each quoted currency as units per EUR. The 30-currency table is attributed to the European Central Bank (ECB), snapshot 4 November 2026; users may edit the snapshot. The ECB explicitly says these reference rates are for information and strongly discourages transaction use. They are not live prices or financial advice.
- Inflation is a compound-rate calculator using the annual percentage entered by the user. It is not a CPI database and does not claim an official historical inflation series.
- Loan, tip, discount, fuel and zakat tools apply the formulas and assumptions stated in their visible notices. They are estimates, not offers, advice or religious rulings.

## Health-related estimates

- The calorie estimator uses the Mifflin–St Jeor resting-energy equation from Mifflin et al., *A new predictive equation for resting energy expenditure in healthy individuals*, American Journal of Clinical Nutrition (1990), [DOI 10.1093/ajcn/51.2.241](https://doi.org/10.1093/ajcn/51.2.241). Activity multipliers (1.2, 1.375, 1.55, 1.725, 1.9) are conventional heuristics, not part of the original Mifflin study; macro split defaults to 25/45/30 and is editable. It is limited to adults aged 18+ and is not a diet prescription.
- Pregnancy dating uses the conventional 280-day estimate from the first day of the last menstrual period; conception-date input is converted using a 266-day estimate. ACOG notes that first-trimester ultrasound is the most accurate method to establish or confirm gestational age and that LMP assumptions can be inaccurate. See [Methods for Estimating the Due Date](https://www.acog.org/clinical/clinical-guidance/committee-opinion/articles/2017/05/methods-for-estimating-the-due-date). The calculator is not prenatal care.
- The water tool uses the explicit illustrative formula `weight kg × lower/upper mL/kg/day × activity multiplier × climate multiplier + extra mL/day`. Lower/upper defaults are 30/35; both multipliers start at 1 and extra fluid at 0. Every assumption is editable. The starting band is contextualized below; user-entered activity/climate factors are not validated medical coefficients or personal drinking recommendations.
- The BAC tool uses a simplified Widmark-style estimate with ethanol density 0.789 g/mL, distribution ratios 0.68 (male) / 0.55 (female), and an illustrative elimination assumption of 0.015 percentage points per hour. The [European Commission road-safety explanation](https://road-safety.transport.ec.europa.eu/eu-road-safety-policy/priorities/safe-road-use/alcohol/archive/acute-impairments-due-alcohol-consumption_en) describes these Widmark ratios and ethanol density; the [Washington State Patrol Widmark equation note](https://www.wsp.wa.gov/breathtest/docs/webdms/Studies_Articles/Widmarks%20Equation%2003-07-2002.pdf) provides an example elimination rate. The NIST [Guidelines for Performing Alcohol Calculations in Forensic Toxicology](https://www.nist.gov/system/files/documents/2022/05/02/OSAC%202020-S-0003_Guidelines%20for%20Performing%20Alcohol%20Calculations%20in%20Forensic%20Tox_REGISTRY%20VERSION.pdf) and Searle's [Alcohol calculations and their uncertainty](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4361698/) explain the forensic context and uncertainty. This simplified tool assumes full absorption and linear elimination and does not account for individual physiology, food, medications or measurement uncertainty. **Never use the estimate to decide whether to drive.**
- BMI and sleep tools retain their visible screening / non-medical disclaimers.

## Time, language and developer utilities

- Time-zone conversion uses the browser's built-in IANA time-zone data through ECMAScript `Intl.DateTimeFormat`. Nonexistent spring-forward wall times are rejected; ambiguous fall-back times use the earlier occurrence and are flagged.
- Holiday dates use Gregorian computus for Easter and explicit calendar rules for the selected region. Users select an IANA reference time zone, defaulting to the browser's zone. The UI shows that zone's reference date and counts calendar dates with the reference day excluded; daylight-saving hours do not alter the day count. The output is a date reference, not legal confirmation of statutory holiday entitlement.
- Morse mappings follow ITU-R [Recommendation M.1677-1: International Morse code](https://www.itu.int/rec/R-REC-M.1677/en).
- QR codes are generated locally with `qrcode-generator@2.0.4`, MIT licensed. The QR content is readable by anyone who scans it.
- SHA-1 and SHA-256 use the browser [Web Crypto digest API](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/SubtleCrypto/digest); MD5 is implemented locally for legacy checksum compatibility only. SHA-1 and MD5 must not be used for security-sensitive purposes. A hash is not encryption.
- HEX/RGB/HSL conversion follows [CSS Color 4, HSL to sRGB](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-color-4/#hsl-to-rgb); RGB channels are quantized to 8 bits. Color contrast uses the WCAG relative-luminance ratio. See [WCAG 2.2, Success Criterion 1.4.3](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#contrast-minimum) and [1.4.6](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#contrast-enhanced).
- JSON validation uses native `JSON.parse` inside a local worker; formatting preserves original numeric tokens, duplicate members, string escapes and string values. Input is bounded at 5,000,000 UTF-16 code units; a conservative 10,000,000-unit output bound prevents pathological indentation expansion. Limit errors explain how to reduce input or use minify mode. Base conversion uses JavaScript `BigInt`.
- Password generation uses browser cryptographic randomness. Strength checking uses the locally bundled [Dropbox zxcvbn](https://github.com/dropbox/zxcvbn) model, version 4.4.2, under its MIT licence. Pattern and strength scores are estimates, not security guarantees.

## Source refresh policy

Recheck date-sensitive snapshots before release. Never label a stored snapshot “live”. If a baseline or official rule cannot be verified, omit it or mark it as user-supplied / illustrative. Human review is required for health notices, locale quality and public-holiday interpretation.

### Population baseline verification state

The official UN WPP2024 Medium CSV and indicator notes were downloaded directly on 5 November 2026. World / Medium `TPopulation1July` values, expressed in thousands, verify **8,231,613,070** people for 2025 and **8,300,678,396** for 2026. The exact reference interval is **365 days**. [The completed audit](docs/POPULATION_AUDIT.md) includes checksums, full selected rows and reproduction instructions. Extrapolation outside that interval remains a simplified estimate.


## Remaining original-tool constants and formula references

- **BMI:** weight in kg divided by height in metres squared. Imperial inputs use the exact factors 1 lb = 0.45359237 kg and 1 inch = 0.0254 m, rather than the rounded conventional factor 703, so equivalent metric/imperial inputs agree. See the NIST factors below and [CDC calculation](https://www.cdc.gov/growth-chart-training/hcp/using-bmi/calculating-bmi.html). BMI is a screening measure, not a diagnosis; this tool does not assign diagnostic categories.
- **Qibla:** bearing uses the great-circle initial-bearing formula. The Kaaba reference coordinate is approximately 21.4225° N, 39.8262° E, rounded from the [German Archaeological Institute (DAI) iDAI.gazetteer entry 1008211](https://gazetteer.dainst.org/doc/1008211.html), licensed CC BY; Google's [Qibla Finder explanation](https://support.google.com/faqs/answer/7364753?hl=en) describes the same great-circle approach. Geolocation remains an explicit user action.
- **Unit and fuel conversions:** length, mass, volume and area constants are based on SI definitions and [NIST SP 811 conversion factors](https://www.nist.gov/pml/special-publication-811/nist-guide-si-appendix-b-conversion-factors/nist-guide-si-appendix-b9). Exact gallon distinctions are retained: US gallon = 3.785411784 L; Imperial gallon = 4.54609 L. Decimal prefixes follow the [BIPM SI Brochure](https://www.bipm.org/en/publications/si-brochure/); binary prefixes follow IEC 80000-13 (Ki, Mi, Gi), so 1000-based and 1024-based units are never conflated.
- **Zakat:** the default editable rate is 2.5%, consistent with [Islamic Relief's zakat rules](https://www.islamic-relief.org.uk/giving/islamic-giving/zakat/zakat-rules/). Eligibility, nisab, lunar-year ownership, debt treatment and school-specific rulings are not calculated; this is arithmetic only, not a religious ruling.
- **Water:** [NICE CG32, recommendation 1.4.2](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg32/chapter/Recommendations#what-to-give-in-hospital-and-the-community) gives a 30–35 mL/kg total-fluid starting range in a specific **adult clinical nutrition-support context**, with qualifications and adjustments for losses and other intake. That context is stated in the UI; it is not generalized into a personal drinking prescription. [EFSA's dietary reference-value explanation](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/topics/topic/dietary-reference-values) distinguishes population reference values from individual recommendations. The product's activity/climate multipliers are neutral defaults of 1, editable assumptions rather than medically prescribed coefficients. Fluid includes food and drinks. Individual clinical guidance takes priority.
- **Holidays:** Easter dates use Gregorian computus. US federal holiday examples are checked against [US OPM](https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/pay-leave/federal-holidays/); England and Wales bank-holiday rules against [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/bank-holidays); Canada examples against [Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/public-holidays.html). Region lists are intentionally incomplete: Germany and Nigeria include a common fixed/Christian subset, and variable lunar holidays, substitute days and province/state exceptions are not comprehensive. Never treat this as a legal calendar.
- **Word/sentence reading estimates:** words use Unicode letters/numbers and combining marks; characters count Unicode code points. Sentence estimates use `Intl.Segmenter` with common English/German abbreviation protection and a Unicode-punctuation fallback. They remain language-dependent estimates, especially for text without word spaces or unfamiliar abbreviations. Reading time uses the explicit product assumption of 200 words per minute, not a universal reading speed.
- **Contemplative practice:** annual and lifetime projections use 52 weeks per year; this is an explicit arithmetic assumption.
- **Sleep cycle:** 90 minutes is a user-adjustable heuristic, not a physiological guarantee.
- **Loan, tip, discount, percentage and recipe:** standard arithmetic formulas; no external rates or prices are supplied. Tip/bill splitting preserves the decimal inputs through exact rational arithmetic before the selected cent rounding; nearest ties round up. Invalid and unrepresentable numeric results are rejected.
- **Statistics:** mean and median avoid unnecessary intermediate overflow; centered/scaled sums produce population and sample variance. Singletons have population variance zero and no sample variance. Input is bounded to 250,000 finite values and 5,000,000 UTF-16 code units; overflowing or falsely underflowed variance results are rejected. Decimal/scientific results still obey JavaScript floating-point precision.
- **Age and date difference:** the reference “today” is the browser's local calendar date. Date-only subtraction uses UTC calendar dates to avoid daylight-saving-hour errors and preserves years 0000–0099 without JavaScript's historical 1900 offset. The UI documents the start-date-exclusive convention.
- **GPA:** the complete illustrative mapping is visible in the tool: A=4, A−=3.7, B+=3.3, B=3, B−=2.7, C+=2.3, C=2, C−=1.7, D+=1.3, D=1, D−=0.7, F=0. Weighted average is Σ(points × credits) / Σcredits. Percentage mode remains separate and is not silently converted to GPA; institutions may use other mappings.


## Deliberate exclusions (scope boundary)

- **AI prediction, fortune-telling and personality/relationship scoring:** excluded because they imply unsupported predictive certainty or misrepresent statistical/AI capability.
- **Medical diagnosis and treatment recommendations:** excluded; the health tools remain limited to explicitly labelled estimates/screening calculations.
- **Live stock/crypto tickers or live currency APIs:** excluded because they require runtime external services and would violate the static local-only architecture. Currency conversion uses a dated offline snapshot.
- **User accounts, cloud profiles and persistent sensitive data:** excluded because they break the static, client-side privacy model.
